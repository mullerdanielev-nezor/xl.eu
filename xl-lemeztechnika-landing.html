About us

Founded in 2019 in Hungary, we've grown into a reliable subcontracting and supply partner for manufacturers across Europe.

Since 2022 our capacity has grown with a new 6000 W flat and tube laser — so we handle every step in-house, from cutting to welding, to EU quality standards at a competitive Hungarian cost base.

Our machinery

  • 12 kW fiber laser – 1500 × 3000 mm (carbon steel up to 40 mm, stainless/aluminum 15–20 mm)
  • 6 kW fiber laser – 1500 × 3000 mm + tube-cutting option (tube: Ø20–220 mm)
  • 2 kW fiber laser – 1500 × 3000 mm
  • 160-tonne edge bender – 3000 mm
  • 220-tonne edge bender – 3000 mm
  • Automatic panel bender
  • Rolling machine – 2000 mm, up to 8 mm thickness
  • CO and TIG welding machines
  • CNC milling and turning machine
  • Automatic fastener press
  • Automatic grinding and deburring machine
  • Stud welder
